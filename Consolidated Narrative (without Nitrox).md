# Confined Open Water Training Program, Narrative Edition

## Day 1 — Init, Simulated Dives 1 & 2

### Paperwork & Admin

- Complete SDI Liability Release & Assumption of Risk and Medical Statement
- Verify prerequisites:
  - Age requirement
  - Parental consent for minors
  - Verify Swim gear:
    - Towel, trunks, wetsuit
    - Mask/fins/snorkel

### Suggested Academic Topics

- Open Water fundamentals: dive physics/physiology, equipment overview, hand signals, buddy system, dive computer use, predive planning.
- Safety & procedures: entries/exits, emergency signals, controlled ascents/descents, neutral buoyancy basics, equipment care.
- Missed academics from final eLearning exams.

### Swim Test

- 200m swim without gear or 300m with mask/fins/snorkel
- 10min survival float/tread
- Breath hold double pool length
- Pike dive
- Buoyancy using lungs as control device

### Dive 1 — Orientation & Core Skills

**Goal:** Comfort, equipment handling, baseline buoyancy control.

**Narrative Sample**

> We are in Roatan, Honduras, preparing for our first boat dive on a Newton, a type of boat with a platform on the back common to North and South America. We are going to dive to a very shallow reef with a MOD of 12fsw. Our dive will last for approximately an hour.
>
> Our goals for this dive are drills and thrills with a focus on safety and initial skill performance. Great luck; our reef has a sandy patch that's 4' deep that we can stand in.
>
> We'll focus on how to:
>
> - Assemble our gear
> - Communicate underwater
> - Dive as a team
> - Manage gas and gear
> - Descend and ascend in a controlled way
> - Work with our regulator
> - Clear our mask
> - Do a basic weight check
> - Fin properly
> - Exit from deep water
>
> All these skills will make our Roatan experience safe and comfortable.

**Pre‑Dive:**

- Briefing: objectives, signals, buddy roles, gas management.
- Equipment assembly and **self-and-buddy predive checks**: verify gas, equipment condition, BCD function, weight placement, mask seal, and communication signals.
- Entry: controlled seated entry and giant stride.

**In‑Water Skills:**

- Surface BCD inflation/deflation, snorkel/regulator exchange.
- **Snorkel skills: adjustment, blast-clear method, regulator/snorkel exchange, surface snorkel swim in full equipment**.
- Controlled descents and ascents.
- **Regulator recovery & clearing at surface and at depth**; alternate air source drill.
- Mask skills: partial and full flood & clear; no‑mask breathing/swim.
- Basic buoyancy: weight check, hover attempts.
- Finning techniques.

**Post‑Dive:**

- Debrief.
- Deep water exit, equipment disassembly.
- Logbook entries.

### Dive 2 — Controlled Descents & Surface Skills

**Goal:** Build confidence with controlled depth transitions, BCD handling at surface, hovering fundamentals.

**Narrative Sample**

> We are in the Maldives, preparing for our second confined open water dive from a dhoni, the traditional Maldivian dive boat with an easy rear entry platform. We'll dive to a shallow lagoon reef with a maximum depth of about 15 feet, and the dive will last roughly an hour.
>
> Our goal for this dive is to build confidence with controlled descents and ascents, practice BCD manipulation at the surface, and develop hovering skills in calm conditions.
>
> We'll focus on how to:
>
> - Perform controlled descents and ascents with simulated safety stops
> - Remove and replace the BCD at the surface
> - Manipulate our weights at the surface
> - Practice regulator recovery and clearing at the surface
> - Begin hovering and trim practice in shallow water
> - Maintain controlled buoyancy throughout the dive
>
> These skills will make your Maldives experience safe and comfortable while preparing you for depth work!

**Pre‑Dive:**

- Briefing: buoyancy control, descent/ascent procedures.
- Equipment assembly & predive checks.
- Entry: backroll.

**In‑Water Skills:**

- Controlled descents and ascents with simulated safety stop.
- Remove/replace BCD at surface.
- Weight system manipulation at surface.
- **Regulator recovery & clearing at surface**.
- Basic hovering and trim practice.
- Fine buoyancy adjustments.

**Post‑Dive:**

- Debrief.
- Exit, equipment disassembly.
- Logbook entries.

## Day 2 — Simulated Dives 3 & 4

### Dive 3 — Depth Work & Emergency Drills

**Goal:** Deepen skills at depth, master BCD manipulation underwater, practice emergency scenarios.

**Sample Narrative**

We are in the Florida Keys, preparing for our third confined open water training dive. We'll dive to a sand patch with a maximum depth of about 30 feet, with clear water and excellent visibility for skill practice. The dive will last roughly an hour.

Our goal for this dive is to build confidence working at depth, manipulate equipment underwater, and practice emergency response drills.

We'll focus on how to:

- Descend and ascend in a controlled manner with simulated safety stops
- Remove and replace the BCD underwater at depth
- Inflate and deflate the BCD orally and with power inflation at depth
- Manipulate our weight system at depth
- Perform a Controlled Emergency Swimming Ascent (CESA)
- Monitor our gauges: SPG pressure, depth, and time
- Practice hovering and trim refinement at depth
- Assist a tired diver and relieve cramps

These skills will make your Keys experience safe and comfortable while building emergency preparedness and depth confidence.

**Pre‑Dive:**

- Briefing: depth protocols, emergency procedures, buddy communication.
- Equipment assembly & predive checks.
- Entry: instructor's choice.

**In‑Water Skills:**

- Controlled descents and ascents with simulated safety stop.
- Remove/replace BCD at depth.
- **BCD oral inflation/deflation and power inflation/deflation at depth**.
- Weight system removal/replacement at depth.
- **Regulator clearing and recovery at depth**.
- **Gauge use fundamentals: monitor SPG pressure, depth, and dive time throughout the dive**.
- Hovering and trim refinement.
- CESA simulation.
- Tired‑diver tow and cramp‑relief techniques.

**Post‑Dive:**

- Debrief.
- Exit, equipment disassembly.
- Logbook entries.

### Dive 4 — Advanced Skills & Environmental Mastery

**Goal:** Complete remaining OW skills, refine buoyancy and navigation, develop environmental awareness.

**Sample Narrative**

We are in the Channel Islands, preparing for our fourth open water training dive from a boat with scenic kelp forests and diverse terrain. Our maximum depth will be about 35 feet, and the dive will last roughly an hour.

Our goal for this dive is to systematize all remaining advanced skills, practice underwater navigation in a realistic environment, and develop environmental awareness for safe, effective diving.

We'll focus on how to:

- Swim without a mask and re-don it underwater
- Execute an out-of-air drill with an alternate air source ascent
- Navigate using natural features and basic compass techniques
- Maintain environmental awareness: avoid contact, hold trim, and use slow, efficient finning
- Manage task-loading while maintaining buoyancy and communication

These skills will make your Channel Islands experience safe and comfortable while preparing you for independent open water diving.

**Pre‑Dive:**

- Briefing: navigation plan, environmental considerations, buddy roles.
- Equipment assembly & predive checks.
- Entry: instructor's choice.

**In‑Water Skills:**

- No‑mask swim and re‑don.
- Out‑of‑air drill with alternate air source ascent.
- **Regulator clearing and recovery: comprehensive surface and depth practice**.
- Navigation basics: natural features and compass techniques.
- Environmental awareness: silt avoidance, trim, slow finning, task-loading.
- Controlled ascents with simulated safety stop.

**Post‑Dive:**

- Debrief.
- Exit, equipment disassembly.
- Logbook entries.

## Continuing Education

### Peak Performance Buoyancy (PPB)

The buoyancy and trim work from Dives 3 & 4 counts toward the SDI Peak Performance Buoyancy specialty. This course is a building block toward your Advanced Open Water certification. To complete PPB:

- Review the PPB eLearning.
- Log additional dives demonstrating advanced buoyancy control (can be completed during your Open Water certification dives or future dives).
- Receive your PPB specialty card; one step closer to Advanced Open Water Diver!

- 20 min: Review & brief; gear assembly.
- 75 min: Dive 3 skills block.

- 75 min: Dive 4 skills block.
- 30 min: Disassembly, debrief, final logbook entries.
